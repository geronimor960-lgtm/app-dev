# My Favorite Anime: Demon Slayer

> "No matter how weak or unworthy you feel, keep your heart burning, grit your teeth and move forward." – Kyojuro Rengoku

## Overview
**Demon Slayer: Kimetsu no Yaiba** is an action/fantasy series following *Tanjiro Kamado* on his quest to cure his sister, Nezuko, and avenge his family.

### Main Characters
1. **Tanjiro Kamado** - *Water & Sun Breathing user*
2. **Nezuko Kamado** - *Demon turned ally*
3. **Zenitsu Agatsuma** - *Thunder Breathing user*
4. **Inosuke Hashibira** - *Beast Breathing user*

### Key Features of the Series
* **Stunning Animation:** Produced by Ufotable.
* **Intense Battles:** High-stakes fights against powerful demons.
* **Emotional Story:** Deep bonds between siblings and comrades.
